# ControlKit User Personas (Real-World Field Profiles)

This document profiles the five core target users for **Money & Profit Control Kit (ControlKit)**. These profiles are drawn from observational research of micro and small business operators in Tier 1 and Tier 2 Indian cities.

---

## Persona 1: Sunita Sharma — Home Baker
- **Age:** 34
- **Location:** Pune, Maharashtra
- **Business:** "Sweet Crumb Studio" (Custom cakes, cookies, festive hampers)
- **Primary Devices:** Vivo Android phone (80% of time) + 7-year-old Dell Windows laptop for weekend admin
- **Languages:** Hindi (spoken at home), English (WhatsApp customer chats, basic accounting)
- **Current Financial Tooling:** 
  - WhatsApp chat search ("paid", "screenshot", "advance")
  - Ruled physical diary where she notes cake weight and price
  - Google Pay & PhonePe transaction history
- **Daily Routine:**
  - 06:30 AM: Check morning ingredient inventory; bake sponges.
  - 11:00 AM: Chat with brides/parents on WhatsApp for custom cake designs. Collect 50% advance via UPI.
  - 04:00 PM: Send delivery via Porter/Dunzo. Ask customer for remaining balance.
  - 09:30 PM: Scroll through bank app trying to remember if the ₹1,800 received at 2 PM was from order #42 or order #45.
- **Top 3 Jobs to be Done:**
  1. Record an order sale and deposit in 3 taps while flour is on her hands.
  2. Know if a customer's balance was actually paid before the delivery driver leaves her doorstep.
  3. Know what her *real* monthly profit is after deducting chocolate, butter, custom boxes, electricity, and Porter delivery fees.
- **Top Fears:**
  - Double-counting advance UPI payments as new revenue when the remaining balance arrives.
  - Losing phone data / accidental app cache clear and losing all past records.
  - Cloud accounting platforms raising monthly subscription prices or locking her out.
- **First Number Checked Every Morning:**
  - Cash + Bank balance total ("Can I buy 5kg Belgian chocolate and pay the dairy supplier today?").

## Persona 2: Rajeshwar Rao — High School Physics Tutor
- **Age:** 48
- **Location:** Hyderabad, Telangana
- **Business:** "Rao Science Academy" (Private tuition batches for 9th-12th standard, ~75 students)
- **Primary Devices:** Samsung Galaxy phone + HP desktop in study room
- **Languages:** Telugu, English
- **Current Financial Tooling:** 
  - Thick spiral register with columns: Student Name, Month, Fee Due, Date Paid, Cash/UPI, Receipt No.
  - Excel sheet updated twice a year when his daughter helps him.
- **Daily Routine:**
  - 05:00 PM – 08:30 PM: Three consecutive teaching batches.
  - Students hand him crumpled ₹500 notes, or parents send UPI with cryptic remarks like "Rohan tuition" or just "fees".
  - 09:00 PM: Crosses out names in register. Frequently forgets whether parent paid for October or November.
- **Top 3 Jobs to be Done:**
  1. See instantly which students have not paid fees for the current month.
  2. Generate a clean, professional fee receipt / invoice with a UPI QR code to WhatsApp to parents.
  3. Reconcile cash collected in his drawer against UPI payments received in his bank.
- **Top Fears:**
  - Looking unprofessional or awkward asking a parent twice for fees when they already paid.
  - Forgetting who paid cash versus who paid UPI.
  - Cloud apps spamming parents or harvesting contact details.
- **First Number Checked Every Morning:**
  - Total pending fees / receivables ("Who do I need to politely remind today?").

## Persona 3: Ramesh Patel — Kirana & Convenience Store Owner
- **Age:** 52
- **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat
- **Business:** "Patel General Stores" (Daily grocery, dairy, snacks, packaged goods)
- **Primary Devices:** Redmi Note Android phone (only device)
- **Languages:** Gujarati, Hindi, rudimentary English numbers
- **Current Financial Tooling:** 
  - Khata diary for regular neighborhood credit customers (Udhaar)
  - Bank passbook printed once a month at the branch
  - Paytm soundbox for UPI announcements
- **Daily Routine:**
  - 06:30 AM: Open shutters, receive Amul milk crates, pay milk distributor cash.
  - 08:00 AM – 01:00 PM: Continuous customer footfall; mix of ₹10 cash, ₹150 UPI.
  - 02:00 PM: FMCG distributors (ITC, HUL, Britannia) arrive for restocking. Pays via cheque or instant UPI.
  - 10:30 PM: Count cash in the wooden cash drawer, tally against expected total.
- **Top 3 Jobs to be Done:**
  1. Fast end-of-day closing routine: count cash drawer, see today's cash vs UPI total, spot missing money.
  2. Record wholesale supplier stock purchases without complex double-entry bookkeeping.
  3. Import HDFC current account CSV statement at month-end to check all distributor payments.
- **Top Fears:**
  - Software that freezes or requires internet when mobile connectivity drops in his shop.
  - Complicated accounting software that forces him to enter journal entries or debit/credit.
  - Tax notices caused by mixing personal household withdrawals with shop expenses.
- **First Number Checked Every Morning:**
  - Yesterday's closing cash drawer balance.

## Persona 4: Ananya Sen — Freelance Brand Designer & Illustrator
- **Age:** 28
- **Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka
- **Business:** "Studio Sen" (Brand identity, packaging design, UI illustrations for startups)
- **Primary Devices:** MacBook Pro + iPhone
- **Languages:** English, Bengali, Hindi
- **Current Financial Tooling:** 
  - Notion page with table of clients and project statuses
  - Free invoice generator website (Canva / Invoice Generator)
  - Google Drive folder with downloaded bank PDFs
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Works on 3-4 retainer clients and occasional one-off packaging projects.
  - Raises invoices at milestones: 50% upfront, 50% on final vector delivery.
  - Irregular cash flow: ₹1,50,000 one month, ₹20,000 the next month.
- **Top 3 Jobs to be Done:**
  1. Create professional Tax Invoices with GSTIN, HSN/SAC code (9983), and intra-state/inter-state IGST calculation.
  2. Track overdue invoices with ageing (30 days, 60 days) to follow up calmly.
  3. Export a clean "Accountant Pack" CSV at quarter-end for her Chartered Accountant to file GSTR-1 and advance tax.
- **Top Fears:**
  - Making a calculation error on GST (e.g. charging CGST/SGST to an out-of-state client instead of IGST).
  - Monthly subscription SaaS tools that cost ₹1,500/month even during lean months.
  - Cloud tools tracking her client roster and revenue data.
- **First Number Checked Every Morning:**
  - Current bank account balance + uncollected invoices total.

## Persona 5: Vikram Chawla — Two-Wheeler Repair & Spares Workshop
- **Age:** 41
- **Location:** Delhi NCR (Mayapuri)
- **Business:** "Speedline Auto Care" (Motorcycle servicing, oil changes, spare parts, accident repairs)
- **Primary Devices:** Lenovo laptop in reception booth + Motorola Android phone
- **Languages:** Hindi, Punjabi, basic English
- **Current Financial Tooling:** 
  - Job card carbon paper pads
  - Manual cash register
  - BharatPe QR stand on the counter
- **Daily Routine:**
  - 09:00 AM: Open garage, take in 8-10 motorcycles, write customer job cards.
  - 11:30 AM: Send mechanic boy to Karol Bagh spare parts market with ₹8,000 cash for disc pads, filters, engine oil.
  - 05:00 PM: Customers collect bikes, pay for labour + parts via UPI or cash.
  - 08:00 PM: Pay 3 mechanics their daily/weekly wages, note down diesel generator fuel.
- **Top 3 Jobs to be Done:**
  1. Distinguish between spare parts stock purchases (COGS/Inventory) and general workshop running expenses (rent, electricity, tea, mechanic wages).
  2. Keep photos/scans of messy paper receipts from local parts wholesalers before ink fades.
  3. Month-end review: know his actual operating profit after paying shop rent and helper salaries.
- **Top Fears:**
  - Losing receipts and parts bills needed to prove expenses to his tax consultant.
  - Mechanics pocketing cash customer payments without job card records.
  - Complicated software that his receptionist or head mechanic cannot operate.
- **First Number Checked Every Morning:**
  - Available operating cash across counter drawer and BharatPe merchant balance.
